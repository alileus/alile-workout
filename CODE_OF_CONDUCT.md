# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Welcome different experience levels, languages and bodies. Critique claims and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and spam are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove harmful contributions or restrict participation. Report concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact options; do not publish private information in an issue. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions and handle reports confidentially where possible.
